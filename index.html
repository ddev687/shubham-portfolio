About me

I like systems that hum quietly at 2M requests a day.

I work across the whole stack: React and Next.js up front, event-driven Node.js, NestJS and TypeScript services behind them, on PostgreSQL, Kafka, Redis and Kubernetes. On the AI side I ship LLM features that hold up under real traffic: structured outputs, retrieval over app data, and a human in the loop. I build resilience in up front (retries, backoff, idempotency, rate limiting) instead of firefighting failures after they land.

I own production end to end, from design and RFCs through deployment and on-call, and I ship daily with Cursor, Claude Code and Copilot. After hours I build my own products. Some of them even have users.

Chapter 03 · Food Market Hub · Singapore, remote

Three weeks to onboard a restaurant. Then one day.

Senior Backend Engineer · Sep 2022 – present

40+ partners → one hub
The problem

Each new customer meant weeks of manual setup. Their POS and accounting tools each spoke a different language.

The fix

Led the integration team behind two-way sync with 40+ partners, 1M+ API calls a day. Moved partner syncs onto queue-based workers with Redis caching, rate limiting, retries and idempotent webhooks, so partner outages stopped causing duplicate writes. Built AI onboarding on the OpenAI API, and tuned PostgreSQL until it cost $500 a month less.
